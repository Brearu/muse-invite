How to redeem it

  1. Open Muse, app or web

    Both paths accept the code. Use the iOS/Android app if you have it, otherwise use the web sign-up page.

  2. Paste the code into the invite field

    It sits on the sign-up form. In the app, look for the redeem or invite entry in your profile. Paste BN5LUW exactly, watch for a trailing space.

  3. Confirm the redemption

    Submitting the code and finishing redemption are two steps. Make sure you get a confirmation, don't assume it went through.

Who this works for

  • Brand-new accounts only. An existing Muse account can't redeem an invite code.
  • US and Canada only. The region check runs against your account or sign-up location.
  • If the code is rejected, it's almost always one of those two things, or the 48-hour window has already closed.

If it doesn't work

The code gets rejected. Why?

Check three things in order: the account is brand new, the region is US or Canada, and you are inside the 48-hour window. Those three cover nearly every failure case.

Where exactly is the input field?

On the web, it's on the sign-up form. In the app, it's in your profile under redeem or invite. Labels shift between versions, so look for the word invite or redeem.
